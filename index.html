Who We Are

An investment partner helping to unlock more capital for transformative impact.

CAPITAL AS A FORCE is a field-based access to finance development partner focused on helping to unlock and deploy capital where it multiplies impact, deepens resilience, and creates long-term value, particularly in high-growth and high-impact markets.

We work with private and institutional capital providers, including foundations, family offices, and development partners, to design and deploy practical investment lenses, catalytic instruments, and investment frameworks that deploy capital for transformative impact.

Our approach is grounded in deep market experience and local context. We believe that capital can be an incredible force for transformation and sustainable growth, especially in Africa and other emerging markets.

[02] Development

Implement sustainable development programs with perspectives that multiply impact.

Fund an SME Market Data Infrastructure
Investors continue to face a recurring problem when they turn to African opportunities: high-quality investment data is unavailable compared to other emerging markets. To assess a deal in Africa, they often have to make numerous phone calls, hire local experts, fly in for on-the-ground research, and collect data themselves. Even with the recent growth of AI research tools, African markets remain so poorly documented online that AI cannot source verifiable information about them. This is a major barrier to capital flowing into the continent, especially as other emerging markets produce high-quality data to justify their investment attractiveness. If you are an investor willing to help solve this problem, partner with us.
